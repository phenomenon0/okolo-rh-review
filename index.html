2. The Fatal Circularity in Theorem 6

Okolo’s manuscript builds five pieces: Part 0 (pp. 1–3) enumerates divisor pairs via the bijection (k, m) ↦ (mk, k)—literally the geometric Sieve of Eratosthenes / Dirichlet’s 1849 divisor hyperbola. Part I (pp. 4–7) notes that the pairing d ↦ x/d reflects exponents β = log d / log x via β ↦ 1 − β, whose fixed midpoint is β = ½. Part III (pp. 8–12) attempts to build a self-adjoint dilation operator whose continuous spectrum is Re(s) = ½. Part IV (pp. 13–16) reproduces Riemann’s classical 1859 Mellin transform of e−πn²t to recover completed zeta ξ(s) = π−s/2Γ(s/2)ζ(s). Part V (pp. 17–22) claims to close the Riemann Hypothesis via Theorem 5.3 ("spiral non-fault-tolerance") and Theorem 6 ("confinement").

What Theorem 5.3 actually proves is an elementary calculus remark: if a mode has envelope xβ = eβu (u = log x) and you change β to β + ϵ (ϵ ≠ 0), the ratio eϵu ≠ 1 alters the function. That holds for every starting exponent β—it says nothing about what the original β equals.

“A fixed object has fixed spectral data: a configuration with a zero off the line is the spectrum of a different object, not of ζ. Hence the zeros are not perturbable and their common real part is a coordinate of a rigid object, not a free parameter; by Theorem 5.3 any other envelope exponent reconstructs a different point set, with an error that grows without bound and cannot cancel, so it is not ζ’s spectrum at all. By Parts I and III the reconstructing exponent of ζ is ½. Therefore every nontrivial zero has real part ½.” — H. P. Okolo, HDF_CRITICAL_EXPONENT.pdf, Theorem 6 (p. 21)

That bolded sentence is literally the Riemann Hypothesis asserted without proof. Neither Part I nor Part III establishes that the zeros of ζ(s) all have real part ½—and Okolo’s own text explicitly admits this on p. 12 (“What is not proved in this part is that the shared value is forced to be ½ for every zero”) and p. 18 (“What Proposition 5.1 does not by itself establish is the location of those common zeros”).

3. Hard Number-Theoretic Counterexamples

ChatGPT’s review illustrated the logical gap with the toy function F(x) = x1/4 + x3/4. In analytic number theory, far stronger genuine zeta-function counterexamples refute Okolo’s entire Parts I–V architecture outright:

  1. Davenport–Heilbronn (1936) & Epstein Zeta Functions: The Davenport–Heilbronn Dirichlet series f(s) (and Epstein zeta functions ζQ(s) of positive definite binary quadratic forms with class number > 1, such as Q(m,n) = m² + 5n²) arise directly from Gaussian lattice heat-kernel Mellin transforms with Jacobi theta inversion and satisfy the exact completed functional equation Λ(s) = Λ(1 − s)—yet have infinitely many zeros off the critical line Re(s) = ½. Using pure Python in verify.py, we independently verified the Davenport–Heilbronn functional equation to 4.6 × 10−16 and located an explicit symmetric off-line zero pair at ρ ≈ 0.8085171825 + 85.6993484854i and 1 − ρ̄ ≈ 0.1914828175 + 85.6993484854i.
  2. Beurling Generalized Primes (Diamond–Montgomery–Vorhauer, 2006): Beurling generalized integers form a free commutative multiplicative monoid—so they satisfy exact unique factorization, generate the exact same divisor lattice and frog bijection (k, m) ↦ (mk, k), obey the exact same divisor involution d ↦ x/d with unique fixed midpoint β = ½, and have a fixed Chebyshev explicit formula—yet Diamond, Montgomery, and Vorhauer proved there exist Beurling prime systems whose zeta functions violate the Riemann Hypothesis.

Closer Look: The Five Compounding Errors in Part III (Pages 8–9)

1. Lemma 3.4 silently deletes the factor u = log x HDF_CRITICAL_EXPONENT.pdf, pp. 8–9

Definition 3.3 sets H = ½(uP + Pu) = −i(u d/du + ½) where u = log x. Lemma 3.4 then writes: “Since d/du = x d/dx under u = log x, the same operator is −i(x d/dx + ½).” Substituting d/du = x d/dx into u d/du yields (log x) x d/dx, not x d/dx.

2. Measure confusion destroys self-adjointness in Theorem 3 HDF_CRITICAL_EXPONENT.pdf, p. 9

The Berry–Keating dilation operator −i(x d/dx + ½) is self-adjoint on Lebesgue space L²(ℝ+, dx) (where the unitary map f(x) ↦ eu/2f(eu) absorbs the +½). Okolo instead places it on Haar space L²(ℝ+, dx/x), where u = log x is directly isometric with no eu/2 factor—leaving H = −i d/du − i/2 with imaginary expectation −i/2. And even after fixing every algebra error to recover Berry–Keating (1999), its spectrum is the entire continuous line ℝ—proving nothing about the discrete zeros of ζ(s).

⟨g, H g⟩L²(dx/x) = ∫−∞∞ g(u) [−i g′(u) − (i/2)g(u)] du = 0 − i/2 = −0.5i ∉ ℝ Exact expectation value for any real unit-norm test function g ∈ Cc∞(ℝ), proving Theorem 3 false on L²(ℝ+, dx/x).

5. Plagiarism Check vs. Levent Alpöge’s Lean-Verified Proof

In his November 24–25 X posts, Okolo alleges that Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge plagiarized his “Primitive” via Claude and “turned it into theta lifts.” Comparing Okolo’s decrypted July 15 PDF directly against Alpöge’s announcement note shows zero mathematical overlap:

Okolo’s Manuscript (July 15, 2026)

  • “Primitive”: Listing multiples (mk, k)—the elementary Sieve of Eratosthenes / Dirichlet’s 1849 divisor lattice.
  • Only mention of “theta”: One single sentence on p. 14 citing Riemann’s 1859 1D Jacobi theta inversion for ∑ e−πn²t.
  • Core argument: Informal 22-page draft (with Part II missing) asserting circularly that spiral sensitivity forces Re(ρ) = ½.
  • Verification status: Fails basic mathematical checks (10 verified errors; no valid RH proof to borrow).

Alpöge’s Proof (Nov 20, 2026)

  • Overarching method: Descendant of Stepanov’s polynomial method for Weil conjectures over finite fields, lifted to ℤ.
  • Theta lifts: Chains of m ≈ √X iterated automorphic theta lifts U(X0) → U(X1) → … → U(Xm) between totally definite unitary groups (Howe’s theta correspondence) mimicking differentiation/multiplicity.
  • Machinery: Kudla seesaw identities, Gross–Gan–Prasad / Bessel periods, 6-fold cover of GL5, Chowla–Selberg & Siegel mass bounds, Bost’s Arakelov slope inequality, Iwasawa towers, weakened Vandiver conjecture.
  • Verification status: 17M-line Lean 4 library independently certified by Lean FRO & Mathlib Initiative via nanoda, con-leche, con-ron, and lean4lean.

Equating Riemann’s 1859 1D Jacobi theta function with chains of √X automorphic theta lifts across unitary groups is purely a verbal pun on the word “theta.”

Summary

  • Did Okolo solve the Riemann Hypothesis? No. Theorem 6 (p. 21) of his decrypted July 15, 2026 Zenodo manuscript (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21381824) begs the question by assuming the reconstructing exponent of ζ(s) is ½. Both Davenport–Heilbronn / Epstein zeta functions (exact functional equation, off-line zeros) and Beurling primes (exact unique factorization, violating RH) refute the proof strategy outright.
